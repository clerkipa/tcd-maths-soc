The Dublin University Mathematical Society

The student mathematical society was established in 1923 by a group of Trinity students including Nobel Laureate Ernest Walton. The purpose of the society is to provide events of mathematical interest for its members, and to encourage the study of mathematics.

What We Do

We try to balance our activities for all maths lovers, whether you want to further you knowledge of maths, or try your hand in competiting in college level competions, or just want to meet people who share an interest in mathematics, we do it all.

If you want to learn about more in-depth in maths than what you might cover in lectures, we regularly have talks from both Trinity lecturers and international speakers about their research and what they specialise in.

If you are looking for a fun evening to make some friends and memories, we have ran events ranging from Countdown to Karaoke to Junior and Leaving Cert Speedruns.

If you want to test your wits and have a bit of a competitive spirit be sure not to miss our annual Integral Bee and visit the weekly problem solving group. You can learn more about them and about intervarsities on the Problem Solving page.
