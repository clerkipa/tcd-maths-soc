The Mathsoc Library

DU Mathsoc has a wide range of mathematical books in its library available for members to browse and borrow. The collection is managed by our librarian Yara Recinella.

To browse some of our collection come to the Mathsoc room and peruse the shelves or if you are looking for a specific title search our catalogue below. Not only do we have a wide range of textbooks to help develop your knowledge, but also books on the history of maths and more lighthearted reads like "Pasta By Design" in case you ever wanted to know what parametric curves define farfalle.

Borrowing books

To borrow one of our books email or if you have other queries regarding the library email Yara at librarian.maths@tcd.ie.
